est_diameter_min and est_diameter_max are perfectly correlated (r = 1.00) — expected, since both are derived from the same magnitude formula. absolute_magnitude has the strongest relationship with diameter (r = -0.56, negative because lower magnitude means a brighter, larger object) — an early hint of the same size-drives-risk pattern the model later confirms.
